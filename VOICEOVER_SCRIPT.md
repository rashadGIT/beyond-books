# Donation Letter Generator - Demo Voiceover Script

## Phase 1: Proof of Concept Demo for Youth Revive Inc.

---

### SECTION 1: Customer Contact List (1-2 seconds)

**[Scene: Excel spreadsheet showing customer contact information]**

"First, we start with your customer contact list from QuickBooks..."

*[Pause to show spreadsheet with names, emails, phone numbers, and addresses]*

---

### SECTION 2: PayPal Donations Data (30 seconds)

**[Scene: Excel spreadsheet showing PayPal donation transactions]**

"...and your PayPal donation transactions. Each transaction includes the donor's name, email, donation amount, fees, and a detailed description of what they donated to."

*[Pause to show scrolling through transactions]*

"Notice how we have multiple donations from the same donors throughout the year—some for general support, others for specific programs like the Building Fund, Youth Programs, or Memorial Gifts."

*[Show variety of donation descriptions and amounts]*

"The system automatically captures all transaction details, including gross amounts, processing fees, and net totals."

---

### SECTION 3: Sales Summary Data (26 seconds)

**[Scene: Excel spreadsheet showing Sales by Customer Summary]**

"We can also work with your QuickBooks Sales by Customer Summary report..."

*[Show simple two-column spreadsheet with customer names and totals]*

"This provides a clean overview of total donations per customer—perfect for generating annual summary letters."

*[Pause to show names and amounts]*

"Whether you're using detailed transaction exports from PayPal, Stripe, or summarized reports from QuickBooks, the system handles them all."

---

### SECTION 4: Application Dashboard - Initial State (5 seconds)

**[Scene: Web application showing empty upload areas]**

"Here's the Donation Letter Generator application. It's clean, simple, and purpose-built for nonprofits."

*[Show the two upload cards: Customer Contact List and Sales by Customer Summary]*

"You'll see two upload areas: one for your customer contact list, and one for your donation data."

---

### SECTION 5: File Upload Process (15 seconds)

**[Scene: File browser showing demo files, selecting Demo_Customer_Contact_List.xlsx]**

"Let's upload the customer contact list first..."

*[Show file selection and upload]*

"The system instantly processes the Excel file..."

**[Scene: Green checkmark appears showing "15 Customers Loaded"]**

"...and confirms fifteen customers loaded successfully."

---

### SECTION 6: Dashboard with Data Loaded (25 seconds)

**[Scene: Dashboard showing stats and data table]**

"Once both files are uploaded, the dashboard comes to life."

*[Show the four stat cards]*

"You can see at a glance: fifteen customers, fifty-eight thousand seven hundred dollars in total donations, zero dollars in fees—since we're using the sales summary—and the same net amount."

*[Show scrolling through the customer table]*

"Below, every donor is listed with their email, phone number, and total contribution amount. All the data is merged and ready to generate official IRS-compliant donation letters."

---

### SECTION 7: Customization & Generation (10 seconds)

**[Scene: Showing "Customize Branding" button and "Generate Letters" button]**

"You can customize the branding with your organization's name, logo, tax ID, and colors..."

*[Hover over Customize Branding button]*

"...then simply click 'Generate Letters' to create personalized tax receipts for every donor."

---

### SECTION 8: Generated Donation Letters (45 seconds)

**[Scene: Letters page showing professional donation letter]**

"And here's the result: beautiful, professional donation letters that are fully IRS-compliant."

*[Show letterhead with Youth Revive Inc. branding]*

"Each letter includes your organization's branding, the official date, and a personalized greeting."

*[Show the recipient section: "To: John Smith, john.smith@email.com"]*

"The letter addresses the donor by name and email..."

*[Show the total contribution box: $1,900.00, Annual Summary 2026]*

"...displays their total contribution in a clear, prominent format..."

*[Show the detailed donation table with dates and descriptions]*

"...and for annual summaries, it breaks down each individual donation by date and description—so donors can see exactly what they supported throughout the year."

*[Show signature section: "Sincerely, Brandie, Executive Director"]*

"Every letter is signed with your authorized signer's name and title, includes your Tax ID, and states that no goods or services were provided in return for the donation."

---

### SECTION 9: Actions & Distribution (25 seconds)

**[Scene: Showing "Send via Email" and "Print Letter" buttons]**

"From here, you have options."

*[Show Send via Email button]*

"If the donor has an email address on file, you can send the letter directly via email with one click."

*[Click button, show green checkmark "Sent"]*

"The system tracks which letters have been sent..."

*[Show Print Letter button for donors without email]*

"...and for donors without email addresses, you can print individual letters or print them all at once."

*[Show the print options]*

"Perfect for mailing physical copies or archiving for your records."

---

### SECTION 10: Closing Summary (15 seconds)

**[Scene: Back to dashboard or letters overview]**

"That's Phase 1: a complete donation letter automation system."

*[Pause]*

"Upload your data, generate IRS-compliant letters, and distribute them—all in minutes instead of hours."

*[Pause]*

"Saving time, reducing errors, and ensuring every donor receives a professional thank-you letter they can use for tax purposes."

### SECTION 11: Call to Action (10 seconds)

**[Scene: Fade to contact info or logo]**

"This is just the beginning. Phase 2 will add automated email campaigns, multi-year summaries, and advanced donor analytics."

*[Pause]*

"Ready to streamline your donor communications? Let's talk."

## Total Duration: ~3 minutes 18 seconds

---

## Notes for Recording:

- **Tone**: Professional, confident, helpful—not salesy
- **Pacing**: Moderate speed, pause on key stats and visuals
- **Emphasis**:
  - "IRS-compliant"
  - Dollar amounts
  - Time savings ("minutes instead of hours")
  - Automation benefits
- **Energy**: Enthusiastic but grounded—this solves a real problem
- **Background Music**: Optional soft, modern corporate background track (keep it subtle)

---

## Key Selling Points Highlighted:

✅ **Simplicity** - Clean interface, easy upload process
✅ **Automation** - Merges data automatically, generates letters instantly
✅ **Compliance** - IRS-compliant donation letters
✅ **Professionalism** - Beautiful, branded letters
✅ **Flexibility** - Works with PayPal, Stripe, QuickBooks
✅ **Efficiency** - Saves hours of manual work
✅ **Distribution** - Email or print options
✅ **Tracking** - Knows which letters were sent
✅ **Scalability** - Handles any number of donors
